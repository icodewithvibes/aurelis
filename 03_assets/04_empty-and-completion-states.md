# 04 — Empty & Completion State Art

Small, calm illustrative/photographic pieces for the moments between action: empty states (no split, no history, rest day) and completion states (session recorded, Forge action done, recovery honored). Tone: quiet encouragement, never nagging, never game-y.

Reference universal rules in `00_asset-strategy.md`.

---

## ES-1 — No Split Imported (onboarding empty)  ·  ESSENTIAL
- **Screen:** Train/Today before any split exists.
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 or 4:5 spot illustration, 1000×1000, centered with padding.
- **Safe text area:** below the art (headline + "Paste your split (ASF)" CTA).
- **Prompt:** *A calm minimalist scene of a single closed chrome-edged notebook resting on cobalt-night ground with one small wildflower beside it, soft silver light, film grain, quiet anticipation, deep negative space, textless, editorial wellness.*
- **Negatives:** universal; no dumbbells/gym clichés, no charts, no game UI, no people.
- **Format/compression:** WebP alpha or on-cobalt WebP; **≤ 60 KB**.
- **Mobile fallback:** a simple SVG crest-outline (CR-0) + text.
- **Essential/optional:** ESSENTIAL.

## ES-2 — Empty Proof Timeline (no history yet)  ·  ESSENTIAL
- **Screen:** Proof before any events.
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1, 1000×1000.
- **Prompt:** *An Unmarked chrome crest silhouette faintly glowing on cobalt-night, a single dormant wildflower bud nearby, soft bloom, film grain, calm and patient mood, textless.*
- **Negatives:** universal; nothing that implies failure or lack.
- **Format/compression:** reuse CR-0 SVG + TX-2 bloom; optional PNG ≤ 40 KB.
- **Mobile fallback:** SVG + gradient.
- **Essential/optional:** ESSENTIAL (can be composed from existing SVG/textures).

## ES-3 — Rest Day / Nothing Planned  ·  OPTIONAL
- **Screen:** Today when no obligation exists.
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 or 1:1.
- **Prompt:** *A wide calm cobalt meadow at dusk, still air, a few closed wildflowers, faint chrome shimmer on the horizon, restful, film grain, soft bloom, textless, spacious.*
- **Negatives:** universal; no urgency, no reminders-as-guilt imagery.
- **Format/compression:** WebP; **≤ 120 KB**.
- **Mobile fallback:** BP-1 backplate reused.
- **Essential/optional:** OPTIONAL.

## CS-1 — Session Recorded (completion moment)  ·  ESSENTIAL
- **Screen:** post-session, over the crest stage (BP-3), synced with the 450–650ms animation.
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 crest stage crop, 1080×1080.
- **Prompt (stage/bloom only — crest is SVG):** *A soft ceremonial silver bloom with one thin restrained prismatic glint arcing across a cobalt-night stage, gentle botanical filigree dissolving at the edges, sacred-calm, film grain, textless.*
- **Negatives:** universal; **no confetti, particles, coins, XP, fireworks, sparkles-as-reward**; keep it restrained and premium.
- **Result copy (not baked in art):** "Proof recorded — N session(s) kept."
- **Format/compression:** composed from CR SVG + TX-2 bloom + TX-4 glint (0 extra KB) or a single ≤ 50 KB WebP.
- **Mobile fallback:** static crest + result line, no animation (reduced-motion parity).
- **Essential/optional:** ESSENTIAL.

## CS-2 — Forge Action Done  ·  ESSENTIAL
- **Screen:** after "Next rep → Done" in Forge.
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1, 800×800.
- **Prompt:** *A single small chrome mark catching a soft silver light on calm cobalt, one faint prismatic glint, understated, film grain, quiet satisfaction, textless.*
- **Negatives:** universal; must stay calm (Forge context can follow a hard moment) — no celebration overload.
- **Result copy:** "Commitment kept."
- **Format/compression:** SVG/textures preferred; optional WebP ≤ 30 KB.
- **Mobile fallback:** static mark + text.
- **Essential/optional:** ESSENTIAL.

## CS-3 — Recovery Honored  ·  OPTIONAL
- **Screen:** when a planned recovery day is recorded.
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1, 800×800.
- **Prompt:** *A softly lit meadow-light scene, a single open wildflower and a calm chrome gleam, restorative warmth over cobalt, gentle bloom, film grain, peaceful, textless.*
- **Negatives:** universal; no "streak saved!" game language in art.
- **Result copy:** "Recovery honored — streak preserved."
- **Format/compression:** WebP ≤ 60 KB.
- **Mobile fallback:** Meadow-Light gradient + text.
- **Essential/optional:** OPTIONAL.

## SF-1 — Forge Safety Mode Visual  ·  ESSENTIAL (calm, non-alarming)
- **Screen:** when `safetyScreen` flags crisis/injury/exhaustion.
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 or backplate wash, subtle.
- **Prompt:** *An extremely calm, soft cobalt gradient with a faint warm silver glow low on the horizon, spacious, gentle, safe and quiet, no drama, film grain, textless.*
- **Negatives:** universal; **no red, no alarm colors, no urgency imagery, no prismatic energy**; nothing that could feel like pressure. Warmth over intensity.
- **Format/compression:** CSS gradient preferred (0 KB) or WebP ≤ 40 KB.
- **Mobile fallback:** flat calm gradient.
- **Essential/optional:** ESSENTIAL (the copy matters most; art must never escalate).

---

### QA
Completion art must never tip into game-reward aesthetics. Safety-mode art must never feel alarming. Empty states must feel patient, not scolding. Verify all result copy sits in a text-safe zone with AA contrast.
