exact prompt. Generate them all in one sitting — generations are slow and the
unlimited window is one day. Tell me when they land and I'll cut them.

duration as noted. Keep the source image's aspect ratio (the backplates are
1080×1910 vertical, which is already correct for App Store previews, TikTok
and Reels).

## Rules I applied writing these

- **One motion per clip.** Seedance is far more reliable asking for one thing.
- **Never ask for text.** Generated lettering is always mangled; titles get
  added in the edit.
- **Never ask for a cut.** One continuous shot per generation.
- **Say what stays still,** not just what moves — that is what stops the model
  wandering off the source image.
- **Loop-friendly on the lift shots,** so a creator can hold on them.
